- **Context-window collapse**: `full-context-s` on the s dataset drops to 23.8% task-avg / 20% overall — the model is overwhelmed by the raw streaming history, confirming that retrieval is necessary.
- **BM25 turn > session**: on the s dataset, turn-level BM25 (83.5% / 80%) outperforms session-level (72.1% / 70%), indicating finer granularity helps sparse retrieval.
- **Dense retrieval (turn) recovers most of the oracle gap**: `rag-dense/turn` on s reaches 95.2% / 92% — matching oracle-level performance, with the 8192-token truncation applied to session texts.
- **Temporal-reasoning is the hardest task** across all systems and datasets (lowest per-type accuracy in nearly every configuration).
